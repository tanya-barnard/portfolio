Case 02 · CRM Audit & Data Quality

Turning CRM mess into a prioritised roadmap.

A structured HubSpot review covering database quality, ownership, duplicate records, reporting, CRM structure, governance and operational improvement opportunities.

12K+Contacts reviewed
5K+Companies reviewed
100+Duplicate companies identified

The challenge

The CRM had accumulated structural and data-quality issues over time. Ownership gaps, duplicate company records and inconsistent processes reduced confidence in reporting and made ongoing CRM management harder.

The brief

Understand the current state, separate symptoms from structural issues, identify high-impact fixes and provide a practical roadmap rather than a generic list of recommendations.

Audit snapshot

From findings to action.
