Case 02 · Customer Operations

From manual post-sale admin to a structured customer operations process.

A connected post-sale operating model across Sales, Client Services, Delivery and Account Management, with the CRM acting as the shared source of truth.

6 stagesPost-sale customer journey
Cross-teamSales, Client Services, Delivery and AM
One updateMultiple coordinated actions

The challenge

Post-sale activity relied too heavily on manual communication, separate updates and individual team knowledge. That made handovers less consistent and reduced visibility once the sale moved into delivery.

The operating principle

Enter the information once. Use it everywhere.

The goal was to capture the right customer and service information in the CRM, then use it to coordinate notifications, tasks, reporting and ongoing lifecycle activity.

Journey design

Clear ownership across every stage.

01 · Sale ClosedSales information is captured and prepared for handover.
02 · Client Services HandoverThe next owner receives the information needed to take over confidently.
03 · Delivery Team TakeoverAssigned team members receive onboarding tasks and required customer context.
04 · Ongoing SupportClient Services and Account Management retain visibility across service delivery.
05 · Changes & GrowthUpgrades, downgrades, additional services and re-onboarding are managed without losing customer history.
06 · ClosureService closure is recorded consistently so reporting remains accurate.
