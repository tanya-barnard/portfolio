Case 05 · Reporting & Dashboards

Reporting built for decisions, not decoration.

Dashboards and reporting structures designed around the questions teams and leadership actually needed answered , supported by cleaner CRM structure underneath.

PipelineCommercial visibility
LifecycleCustomer progression
ManagementKPIs & operational visibility

The principle

A dashboard can look polished and still be wrong. Reliable reporting depends on consistent CRM structure, properties, ownership and lifecycle logic. I treat the data model and the report as one system.

Reporting areas

  • Sales pipeline
  • Deal performance
  • Customer lifecycle
  • Conversion
  • Team activity and performance
  • Client Services / onboarding visibility
  • Management KPIs
Reporting framework

Start with the business question.

QuestionWhat does leadership or the operational team actually need to know?
DefinitionAgree what the metric means and which records should count.
DataCheck whether the underlying CRM structure can support that definition reliably.
ReportBuild the report or dashboard using consistent logic.
AdoptionMake the output easy enough for teams to use without reinterpreting it every time.

What I worked on

  • Dashboard design and rebuilds
  • Pipeline and lifecycle reporting
  • Management visibility
  • Data cleanup that improved report trust
  • Reporting logic tied to CRM redesign work

What this demonstrates

  • Ability to translate business questions into CRM reporting
  • Understanding of the data dependencies behind dashboards
  • Cross-functional reporting perspective
  • Focus on usable management information rather than vanity metrics
