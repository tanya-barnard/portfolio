Case 03 · Workflow Automation

Automation built around the process , not the other way around.

More than 50 HubSpot workflows built and managed across Sales, Client Services and internal operations, supporting lifecycle progression, handovers, record updates, routing and operational consistency.

50+Workflows built & managed
Cross-teamSales + Client Services + internal ops
Lifecycle-ledAutomation tied to actual process stages

The operational problem

Manual updates, repetitive admin and inconsistent handovers can quickly create gaps in CRM data. The goal was not simply to add more workflows, but to use automation where it reduced friction and improved consistency.

What the workflows supported

  • Lifecycle progression
  • Deal and record updates
  • Internal notifications
  • Task creation and follow-up
  • Sales-to-service handovers
  • Re-onboarding and commercial changes
  • Data consistency and reporting support
Workflow logic

Trigger → decision → action → visibility.

A simplified representation of how I approach operational automation.

Trigger

  • Lifecycle or Deal change
  • Property condition
  • Form or customer action
  • Operational milestone
→

Outcome

  • Update the right record
  • Route or notify the right person
  • Create the next task
  • Keep reporting logic consistent

How I evaluate automation

  • Does it solve a real operational problem?
  • Is the enrollment logic clear and maintainable?
  • What happens when data is missing or incorrect?
  • How does it affect downstream reporting?
  • Who needs visibility when something changes?

What this demonstrates

  • Hands-on HubSpot workflow design
  • Process thinking before automation
  • Cross-functional handover design
  • Awareness of data and reporting dependencies
  • Ability to maintain automation at scale
