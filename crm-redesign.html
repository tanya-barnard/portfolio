Case 01 · CRM Architecture

Contact-led CRM → Deal-based customer lifecycle

A structural redesign to reduce duplication, improve customer history, support repeat sales journeys and make reporting more reliable.

Full redesignContact-led → Deal-based model
Cross-functionalSales + Customer Success
LifecycleSale → Onboarding → Delivery → AM

The challenge

Sales and Customer Success activity was being managed primarily through Contact records. This created duplicate records, fragmented histories, reporting limitations and poor visibility across repeat purchases, upgrades and re-onboarding.

What I changed

  • Moved sales and customer journeys to Deals.
  • Kept the Contact as the central customer record.
  • Migrated historical activity.
  • Rebuilt workflows, dashboards and reporting logic.
  • Designed an end-to-end lifecycle structure.
