Case 02 · CRM Audit

Turning CRM mess into a prioritised roadmap

A structured review of data quality, ownership, duplicates, reporting, governance and automation opportunities across a large HubSpot portal.

12K+Contacts reviewed
5K+Companies reviewed
100+Duplicate companies identified

The challenge

The portal had grown without enough governance. Ownership gaps, duplicate records, inconsistent structure and reporting issues were reducing confidence in the CRM.

What I changed

  • Reviewed Contacts, Companies and Deals.
  • Assessed ownership, duplicates and database quality.
  • Reviewed reporting and CRM structure.
  • Identified governance and automation gaps.
  • Produced findings, priorities and a phased optimisation roadmap.
